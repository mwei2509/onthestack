# Combat damage division follows what players say

The proposal assigned a blocked attacker's damage "lethal to the blockers in order". Damage assignment order is obsolete in the current rules: the attacker divides damage among its blockers however they like, and only trample requires lethal damage on every blocker before the rest goes to the player (CR 510.1c, 702.19b).

The engine shows a default division in the combat preview (one blocker gets everything; with several, lethal to each in turn, the rest to the last or through to the player with trample). What players say overrides it, either as a division ("4 to the Spider, 2 to the Wall") or as an outcome the engine works backwards from ("the Spider dies, the Wall survives"). Nothing waits for an answer.
