# "Not right" on a readback note opens Say what happened, until skipping triggers

Status: Accepted (decided with Melissa, 2026-11-27, in the session grilling #173)

Skipping a trigger (ADR 0058) needs #49. Until it lands, "Not right" on a readback note opens "Say what happened…", as on a card (ADR 0128). Once #49 lands, "Not right" on a trigger's note skips the trigger. Notes for combat damage and for spells and abilities players cast keep opening "Say what happened…", since skipping applies to triggers.
