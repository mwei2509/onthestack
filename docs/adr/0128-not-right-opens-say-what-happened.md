# "Not right?" on a card opens Say what happened, until BoardCorrections

Status: Accepted (decided with Melissa, 2026-11-24, in the session planning #170)

The card sheet on the battlefield screens has "Not right?" (canvas decision 27). BoardCorrections by tap are #51 and aren't built yet. Until they are, "Not right?" opens the same box as "Say what happened…", where the player says the fix ("Goblin Guide is tapped", "the Bolt didn't resolve") and the interpreter re-reads the turn. A Shared screen opens it for the field's player, as "Say…" on a seat does (ADR 0107). Once #51 lands, "Not right?" opens a BoardCorrection instead.

Hiding "Not right?" until #51 was rejected: players would see a wrong card with no way to fix it from there.
