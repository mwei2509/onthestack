# The interpreter's questions appear in the readback tray

Status: Accepted (decided with Melissa, 2026-11-23, in the planning session for #43).

Amended by ADR 0114 (an answer keeps the question it answers).

The interpreter's PlayerQuestions (ADR 0043) appear in the readback tray of ADR 0057, labelled with the player they ask and shown on every screen. Showing a question only on that player's phone comes with M2.

The player answers by typing, or by tapping one of the question's options, which sends that option's text as their next PlayerStatement. An answer is an ordinary statement, so the next re-read settles the question and stops returning it. Options are readings of what was said, never legal plays (principle 4).
