# Cards on the field show the counters the app has

Status: Accepted (decided with Melissa, 2026-11-24, in the session planning #170)

Settles the open question on the canvas's battlefield row. When the engine has counters on a permanent, its card shows them as a small badge ("+1 ×2"), and its sheet spells them out ("Two +1/+1 counters"). Loyalty shows in the card's corner. The engine only has counters someone said or corrected, so most cards show none. Seeing what the app has lets the table check it against the physical board (principle 5).

Hiding counters, because players track their own, was rejected: the engine's power/toughness already includes counters, and a 3/3 printed as 1/1 needs its reason on screen.
