# Yes/no questions ask one thing

Status: Accepted (decided with Melissa, 2026-11-24, after "Did you play a second land, or was it a repeat?" came with Yes/No buttons)

A `yesNo` PlayerQuestion asks one thing that "yes" or "no" answers ("Did you play a second Godless Shrine?"). An either/or question is a `chooseOption` with both readings as its options. The interpreter's prompt says so.

## Consequences

- The quick answers on a yes/no question (Yes, No) always mean something.
